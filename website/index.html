Research results

One preparation rule, many geometries.

Every finite simple graph occurs as the support of mutual information in a separable diagonal quantum state. With the same subsystem count and local dimension, a path and a triangle give distinct recovered relations. Specified continuum families also yield different spatial dimensions.

Analytic result

Independent fair bits copied to endpoints yield exactly two bits of mutual information on an edge and zero off the graph. The preparation needs no physical embedding.
