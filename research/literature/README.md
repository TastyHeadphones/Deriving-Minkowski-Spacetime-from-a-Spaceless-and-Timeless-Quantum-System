| Topic | Primary source | Structure already present |
|---|---|---|
| Relational time | D. N. Page and W. K. Wootters, “Evolution without evolution: Dynamics described by stationary observables” (1983). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.27.2885) | Clock subsystem, clock observable and system dynamics enter the construction. |
| Wheeler–DeWitt constraint | B. S. DeWitt, “Quantum Theory of Gravity. I. The Canonical Theory” (1967). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.160.1113) | Canonical gravity starts with spatial metric variables and gravitational constraints. |
| Thermal time | A. Connes and C. Rovelli, “Von Neumann algebra automorphisms and time-thermodynamics relation in generally covariant quantum theories” (1994). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/0264-9381/11/12/007), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9406019) | State and observable algebra are supplied. Identifying modular flow with physical time is a hypothesis. |
| Modular theory | M. Takesaki, *Tomita's Theory of Modular Hilbert Algebras and Its Applications* (1970). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/BFb0065832) | Algebra plus cyclic and separating vector in the usual formulation, or a suitable faithful state. No physical calibration follows from modular theory alone. |
| Causal sets | L. Bombelli, J. Lee, D. Meyer and R. D. Sorkin, “Space-time as a causal set” (1987). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.59.521) | Partial order and local finiteness are microscopic inputs. Manifoldlike behavior is further structure. |
| Algebraic quantum field theory | R. Haag and D. Kastler, “An Algebraic Approach to Quantum Field Theory” (1964). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1704189) | Local algebras are assigned to regions of a chosen spacetime. |
| Entanglement and geometry | M. Van Raamsdonk, “Building up spacetime with quantum entanglement” (2010). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10714-010-1034-0), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.3035) | Uses an AdS/CFT setting with a boundary theory and geometric dual. |
| Tensor networks and holography | B. Swingle, “Entanglement Renormalization and Holography” (2012). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.86.065007), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/0905.1317) | Renormalization network and a comparison to holographic geometry. |
| Ryu–Takayanagi | S. Ryu and T. Takayanagi, “Holographic Derivation of Entanglement Entropy from the anti–de Sitter Space/Conformal Field Theory Correspondence” (2006). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.96.181602), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0603001) | A geometric bulk dual, spatial boundary regions and extremal-area prescription are used. |
| ER=EPR | J. Maldacena and L. Susskind, “Cool horizons for entangled black holes” (2013). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/prop.201300020), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.0533) | Grounded in gravitational and holographic examples. The general proposed relation is conjectural. |
| Lieb–Robinson bound | E. H. Lieb and D. W. Robinson, “The finite group velocity of quantum spin systems” (1972). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01645779) | Uses lattice and interaction hypotheses with Hamiltonian dynamics. |
| Emergent Lorentz invariance | M. M. Anber and J. F. Donoghue, “The emergence of a universal limiting speed” (2011). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.83.105027), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.0780) | Renormalization-group models start with fields on spacetime and compare speeds. |
| Causal reconstruction | D. B. Malament, “The class of continuous timelike curves determines the topology of spacetime” (1977). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.523436) | Begins with time-oriented Lorentzian spacetimes and causal distinguishability hypotheses. The causal structure determines geometry only up to conformal freedom. |
| Operational reconstruction | J. Ehlers, F. A. E. Pirani and A. Schild, “The Geometry of Free Fall and Light Propagation” (1972 reprint). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10714-012-1353-4) | Uses event manifolds and distinguished families of light rays and free-fall trajectories. |

The clock argument is a finite variant of the Page–Wootters construction. No-signalling, Lorentz interval invariance and positive conformal invariance are standard results. The common graph preparation and its role as a countermodel to the stated reconstruction claim are the focus of this project. Priority for the construction has not been established.
