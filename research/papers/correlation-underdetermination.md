## Abstract

Every finite simple graph is the support of pairwise quantum mutual information in a separable diagonal state of finite registers. The construction works with a fixed local Hilbert dimension for all graphs on the same number of labels. The correlation support therefore fails to select even a finite relational geometry. Families using the same preparation and reconstruction rules have controlled continuum limits of arbitrary positive integer dimension with the Manhattan metric. These findings constrain a specified inference from quantum data. They do not exclude additional nongeometric postulates that single out a relativistic sector.

## Assumptions and scope

**Q0.** Finite quantum kinematics allows a nonzero finite complex Hilbert space and a positive trace-one density operator. Its only intrinsic equivalence here is unitary conjugation. Q0 has no subsystem factorization, event algebra, clock observable, dynamics, embedding or spacetime labels. A basis in a proof can be a representation without being physically privileged.

**Q1.** In the correlation models we additionally supply a finite set of subsystem labels, a tensor factor for each label, and a state diagonal in the product of chosen local bases. These are quantum subsystem and measurement choices, with no embedding or adjacency supplied to the reconstruction. They are stronger than Q0. Different preparation instances can use different states. Graphs in the existence proof index examples, and no graph is assumed to be physical space.

**R1.** For distinct labels the reconstruction puts an undirected edge wherever reduced-state mutual information is positive. It uses unit-length walks to reconstruct distance. This is an explicit mathematical prescription applied to the state and factorization, not part of Q0 or a claim that mutual information universally measures distance.

Q0 alone has a one-dimensional model, with density 1 and scalar algebra. Its only unital subalgebra and state are trivial, so any reconstruction that requires distinct operational events or distinguishable clock readings is unavailable in that model. In a maximally mixed state, the eigenbasis is nonunique and the stabilizer is the entire unitary group. Intrinsic equivalence alone does not privilege a particular factorization or observable there. A rule can still *assign* a fixed external geometry to every input, so a literal impossibility of defining any map to Minkowski space would be false. A derivation needs a specified link to operational quantum data.

## Every graph occurs

**Theorem 1.** Fix a positive integer $N$. For every simple graph $G=(V,E)$ on $N$ labels there exists a separable diagonal state on $(\mathbb C^{2^{2(N-1)}})^{\otimes N}$ whose mutual information for two different labels is two bits on edges and zero elsewhere.

**Proof.** For each ordered pair $(v,w)$ whose unordered pair is an edge, draw one independent fair bit $B_{vw}$. Write it into distinct register slots at both $v$ and $w$. Each label uses $2\deg(v)$ slots. Pad to $2(N-1)$ slots with deterministic zeros and take the diagonal mixture of the induced product basis states. This is a convex combination of projectors onto product vectors, hence has positive quadratic form, trace one, and is separable. Swapping register coordinates or consistently relabeling sources does not affect the entropy computation.

Let $T_v$ be the set of tokens read by $v$. A collection of $k$ independent fair bits has $2^k$ equiprobable configurations and entropy $k$ in bits. Each register determines all distinct bits that enter it and vice versa, with padding fixed. The joint register similarly determines the bits in $T_v\cup T_w$. Reduced density matrices are diagonal with the resulting classical probabilities. The quantum mutual information is therefore
$$
I(v:w)=|T_v|+|T_w|-|T_v\cup T_w|=|T_v\cap T_w|.
$$
For distinct endpoints, any shared token has exactly these endpoints. When there is an edge, its two orientations give exactly two tokens. Otherwise the intersection is empty.

The construction can also pad with independent fair private bits. This makes the single-label marginals uniformly mixed at the same local dimension and leaves all mutual information values unchanged, since no padding source is shared. Thus even identical single-label marginals do not determine the reconstructed relation.

**Corollary 2.** Within Q0, Q1 and R1, there is no unique isomorphism type of reconstructed adjacency, even restricting to three labels, equal local dimensions, and connected graphs. The path and triangle both occur. The triangle relates every distinct pair, and the path excludes its endpoints. A bijection preserving edges would have to preserve completeness, which is impossible. Allowing the edge-free graph gives no finite interlabel distance. A graph with one edge and an isolated vertex gives disconnected components; the path metric on a graph with a branching vertex need not resemble a manifold neighborhood at the scale of graph edges.

This negates a universal claim about R1 on this class. It does not negate all other, operationally specified reconstruction rules or assumptions. The Lean development verifies the token-support equivalence, the finite nonisomorphism and their combination, and positivity of a normalized diagonal model. The tensor-state entropy computation above is an analytic proof.

## Distances, topology and dimension

In a connected component, define $d(v,w)$ as the least number of edges in a finite walk between the labels. Nonnegativity follows from nonnegative lengths. The empty walk realizes distance zero exactly when the endpoints agree. Reversing a walk preserves length and exchanges the endpoints, giving symmetry by minimizing in both directions. Concatenating two minimizing walks gives a walk with the sum of their lengths and proves the triangle inequality. R1 gives an extended metric with $+\infty$ across components. The finite positive off-diagonal distances induce the discrete topology. A direct prescription such as inverse mutual information would be undefined on zeros and has no triangle inequality from Q1. For example, the path gives finite inverse mutual information between neighbors and infinite inverse mutual information between the endpoints.

For each integer $d\ge1$, consider finite graph examples with label sets $\{-m^2,\ldots,m^2\}^d$ and edges when one integer coordinate changes by one. These are abstract graphs in the model, with no embedded lattice in the microscopic axioms. Only in the reconstruction and limiting comparison use the map $k\mapsto k/m$ into a benchmark metric space. The rescaled walk distance equals the $\ell^1$ distance of mapped points because a monotone coordinate walk always stays in the box and every edge can reduce the coordinate discrepancy by at most one. On any fixed bounded region and for large $m$, floor sampling $u\mapsto\lfloor mu\rfloor$ has $\ell^1$ positional error at most $d/m$. Comparing two positions gives a distance error at most $2d/m$ by two triangle inequalities. This proves locally uniform convergence of sampled pair distances and density on every bounded region to complete, connected $(\mathbb R^d,\|\cdot\|_1)$. The stated bound also controls perturbations of the comparison points by adding their two positional errors. It is a selected family, with no claim of universality under perturbed states or new reconstruction maps. A threshold using strictly positive mutual information is sensitive to turning a zero into a positive value. A fixed threshold in $(0,2)$ is stable in finite models when all pairwise errors are smaller than the lesser of the two margins.

The infinite grid has ball size $\sum_{k=0}^{\min(r,d)}2^k\binom{d}{k}\binom{r}{k}$: choose $k$ nonzero coordinates, their signs, and positive magnitudes adding to at most $r$. Its volume-growth dimension is $d$. Uniform probability over the ball has entropy $\log_2 |B(r)|$, so the corresponding logarithmic information-growth dimension also tends to $d$. The benchmark has the usual topological dimension $d$ because the $\ell^1$ and Euclidean norms define the same topology. Each finite label space's covering dimension is zero. A separate spectral example places a chosen random walk on the infinite grid with each coordinate updated independently by $-1,0,1$ of probabilities $1/4,1/2,1/4$. The one-axis return probability is $\binom{2s}{s}/4^s$ at step $s$, from the coefficient of zero in $(z^{-1}+2+z)^s/4^s$. Independence and Stirling's formula give the $s^{-d/2}$ return exponent used to define spectral dimension. That choice of walk is extra structure. Matrix size, number of tensor factors, and number of distinguishable messages do not pick any one of these definitions.

For $d=1$ the limit is Euclidean as a metric; for $d\ge2$ the $\ell^1$ limit fails the parallelogram identity and does not have a Euclidean norm metric. The models realize arbitrary integer dimensions and do not select three. These benchmark limits use geometry to *compare* reconstructed objects after the fact. They do not assume continuity or dimension for general Q1 states.

## Conditioning, modular flow and temporal orientation

Add a chosen finite clock with orthonormal basis $|j\rangle$, a cyclic permutation $S|j\rangle=|j+1\rangle$, a unitary $U$, and a normalized vector $\phi$ with $U^m\phi=\phi$. The history $|\Psi\rangle=m^{-1/2}\sum_j|j\rangle\otimes U^j\phi$ is invariant under $S\otimes U$. Conversely, expanding any invariant vector as $\sum_j |j\rangle\otimes\phi_j$ and comparing coefficients gives $\phi_{j+1}=U\phi_j$. All norms are equal by unitarity, and for a normalized history the conditioned rays are therefore defined at each label. This is a Page–Wootters-style discrete construction [1]. The same stationary equation admits $U=I$ and hence no change. Reversing both $S$ and $U$ preserves the constraint on the same history, so the construction does not select temporal orientation. The numerical illustration uses real rotations by $\pi/m$ and the global phase $e^{i\pi/m}$ per step can make $U^m=I$ without changing the displayed probabilities.

In an independently chosen real-parameter model, $U(s)=\exp(-isH/\hbar)$ for a self-adjoint $H$ gives $i\hbar\,d\phi_s/ds=H\phi_s$ on differentiation in finite dimension. An internal clock must still physically calibrate the parameter $s$ and support repeatable conditional predictions. The spectrum of a finite unitary does not fix a unique continuous generator. A cyclic order also has no global linear before/after relation. A stable clock, time orientation and duration are separate questions.

For a faithful finite density matrix the map $\sigma_s(A)=\rho^{is}A\rho^{-is}$ is an algebra automorphism group; the exponent parameter $s$ is real and dimensionless. For a tracial state this flow is trivial. For a Gibbs state it equals conjugation by $\exp(-i\beta sH)$ in this convention, and matches a Heisenberg parameter $t=-\hbar\beta s$ once a thermal interpretation and calibration are supplied. The thermal time hypothesis proposes a physical interpretation for such a state/algebra-derived modular flow [2, 3], and neither the finite identity nor Tomita–Takesaki theory selects an operational clock or time direction in Q0.

## Interventions and propagation

For a bipartite density $\rho_{AB}$ and a trace-preserving channel on $A$ with Kraus operators $K_j$, the remote expectation of every $B$-observable $M$ is unchanged. In finite dimension, cyclicity of the trace gives
$$
\sum_j\mathrm{Tr}\!\left[(K_j\otimes I)\rho_{AB}(K_j^\dagger\otimes I)(I\otimes M)\right]
=\mathrm{Tr}\!\left[\rho_{AB}\left(\sum_j K_j^\dagger K_j\otimes M\right)\right]
=\mathrm{Tr}[\rho_{AB}(I\otimes M)].
$$
This applies even to entangled states. Conditioning on a selective outcome can change the remote conditional state, and learning that outcome requires a separate channel. In this work a signalling relation requires a choice of intervention that changes the receiver's unconditional outcome law. Statistical dependence and entanglement alone do not establish that relation. Quantum state transfer requires additional channel access. Q0 does not specify the interventions, accessible measurements, or allowed channel compositions.

An optional directed, layered protocol permits a bit to move across one graph edge per update, with waiting allowed. An intervention at label $u$ can then affect $v$ after $n$ updates exactly when $d(u,v)\le n$. Induction shows each reached label has a walk of length at most $n$, and a shortest walk with waiting realizes the converse. The layered reachability relation on labels and integer rounds is a partial order: the round is monotone and a zero-round path fixes the label, concatenation gives transitivity, and waiting gives reflexivity. It is introduced through a controlled protocol, not inferred from the static state. The speed bound is one edge per round relative to that protocol. Identity channels give zero propagation, unrestricted direct channels give no distance-controlled bound, and parallel updates at different rates in uncoupled sectors give different slopes. Lieb–Robinson bounds [4] likewise require locality or quantitative decay with respect to a selected interaction structure and a dynamics. They are not alone a Lorentz symmetry or an exact invariant speed.

## Symmetry, signature and reconstruction

The same static data can be combined with different protocols. Coordinate translations and $x'=x-vt$, $t'=t$ illustrate Galilean kinematics with absolute time and no finite invariant signal cone. The transformation $x'=x$, $t'=t-bx$ in one spatial dimension illustrates Carrollian kinematics preserving vertical causal directions and allowing only spatially stationary propagation in the degenerate model. Zero dynamics or independently chosen update permissions are compatible with Q1. These transformations describe conditional comparison models with extra kinematics. Q0 does not establish geometric realizations of either one.

For comparison, assume a flat affine Lorentzian form, a common null cone, calibrated rods and clocks, and a proper, orthochronous collinear inertial change of coordinates fixing the origin. In the $ct,x$ plane, null coordinates $u=ct+x$, $w=ct-x$ have product $c^2t^2-x^2$. The transformation preserves each null line and hence takes $(u,w)$ to $(a u,a^{-1}w)$, with $a>0$ by time orientation and determinant fixed by interval preservation. The primed spatial origin $x'=0$ moves at $x=vt$, so $a(1+v/c)=a^{-1}(1-v/c)$. Substitution gives $\gamma=(1-v^2/c^2)^{-1/2}$, $x'=\gamma(x-vt)$ and $t'=\gamma(t-vx/c^2)$ for $|v|<c$. Transverse coordinates may be fixed. The common cone is shared by assumption, with $c$ the conversion between time and length units. A one-frame propagation bound alone does not establish any of these inertial symmetries or its universality.

A causal reconstruction may recover order. With appropriate differentiable, causal-distinguishing Lorentzian manifold hypotheses, causal relations can determine conformal structure [5]. These hypotheses must be independently recovered before applying a reconstruction theorem. Multiplying a Lorentzian metric by a smooth positive factor preserves its null directions and causal curves but generally changes lengths and curvature. Even a constant factor changes the values on specified tangent vectors, although constant rescalings of all Minkowski space are abstractly isometric by a dilation. Order alone cannot calibrate the metric on the original labeled events. A volume element fixes a positive conformal factor in a known dimension, since $dV_{\Omega^2g}=\Omega^n dV_g$. A complete flat Lorentzian cylinder from a spacelike translation quotient has locally the Minkowski interval and different topology. Local signature, causal order and flatness alone do not supply global completeness, topology or the required global isometry.

## Consequences for stronger axioms

* Rich distinguishable subsystems are needed for the selected operational interpretation and exclude the scalar model. They do not exclude any graph or imply dimension or dynamics. Connectedness eliminates disconnected examples and leaves both the path and triangle.
* A family with nonvanishing continuum density and volume-growth exponent three would eliminate the explicit $d\ne3$ grid families relative to that reconstruction. Imposing that exponent is already dimension-selecting. Even at exponent three, these families give a spatial norm without a Lorentzian spacetime.
* An intervention algebra and an internal ordering are needed to formulate an operational causal relation in this framework. An assigned graph by itself does not determine either one. A common nonzero cone for every sector and every inertial observer would explicitly supply the desired universality unless independently derived from nongravitational microscopic axioms.
* Calibrated volume or proper-time information can resolve conformal-scale freedom once a differentiable Lorentzian geometry is available. Flatness, global topology, and completeness still need proofs.
* A selected continuous relativistic sector is open. No minimal sufficient independent axiom set has been identified. Countermodels establish insufficiency of the stated weaker assumptions, while no independence claim is made without a pair of models or a separate proof.
